# Source map

Primary source: Josef Muller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems in Graphic Design: A Visual Communication Manual for Graphic Designers, Typographers and Three Dimensional Designers*. The supplied scan is bilingual English/German. The linked copy is available at [Internet Archive](https://ia903105.us.archive.org/29/items/GridSystemsInGraphicDesignJosefMullerBrockmann/Grid%20systems%20in%20graphic%20design%20-%20Josef%20Muller-Brockmann.pdf).

## Interpretation boundary

The book's specific point sizes, paper standards, and production examples reflect its print era. The skill preserves the relationships—measure, interval, alignment, hierarchy, proportion, and repeatability—while requiring the practitioner to re-evaluate accessibility, device behavior, interaction, and modern production constraints.
