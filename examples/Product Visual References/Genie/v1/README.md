## How to use this set

Use these approved compositions as creative references. Paper is the authoritative editable source; PNGs are outputs. Keep v1 intact and duplicate the relevant composition for future revisions. The review board is a historical checkpoint, not a second active master.

The clean exports omit the surrounding review notes and source columns. The expanded Thinking, Ontology and Permission Checks assets intentionally retain their editorial-sequence labels. Keep those labels visible when using a full sequence. Other assets are editable reconstructions of supplied promotional imagery, not authenticated live product captures; retain that distinction in the surrounding creative context. Depicted numbers, recommendations and outcomes are demo content, not current business claims. None of the portrayed actions were executed.

Preserve original wording, data, hierarchy and asset identities when using these as faithful references. For a new story, work in a separately named derivative and label new sample data or proposed behavior. Do not stretch the compositions. Use the dense document at full 1920px width, or focus on a labeled detail crop; do not rely on its body text in a small thumbnail.
